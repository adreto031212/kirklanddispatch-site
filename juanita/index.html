The latest in Juanita

November 12, 2026

Juanita High School, 10601 NE 132nd Street, is asking to amend its campus master plan for a two-story, roughly 9,500-square-foot addition on the north side of the fieldhouse, with a new entry atrium and locker room (ZON26-00515). The Hearing Examiner holds a hybrid hearing Thursday, November 19, at 10 AM; written comments are due by 8 AM that day to planner Tony Leavitt, TLeavitt@kirklandwa.gov. Notice of hearing

November 5, 2026

Crews are replacing stormwater pipe on NE 116th Street near 101st Place NE and 104th. Expect traffic control from 9 AM to 3 PM on weekdays, with bicycles merging into traffic, flaggers, or traffic shifted to the center lane. The work is expected to run into November. City traffic alerts

October 29, 2026

The Kirkland Turkey Trot is Sunday, November 15, at Juanita Beach Park, 9703 NE Juanita Drive: the 10K starts at 9 AM, a quarter-mile kids' run at 9:05, and 5K waves at 9:15 and 9:18. Both courses start and finish in the park. Race page

October 22, 2026

Two dates on council's calendar: the Juanita Pathway Feasibility Study on Tuesday, November 17, and the Juanita High School development plan amendment, a quasi-judicial item, on January 5. Council calendar

October 15, 2026

The 2026 overlay program has crews on NE 134th, 136th and 137th Streets between 90th and 100th Avenues NE, with intermittent disruption 7 AM to 6 PM and permanent markings due this month. Saturday morning, eastbound NE Juanita Drive closes 7 to 11:30 AM for the half marathon. City traffic alerts · Half marathon alert

October 8, 2026

Saturday, October 17, the half marathon closes eastbound NE Juanita Drive between 93rd and 98th Avenues NE from 7 to 11:30 AM; cars on the course get towed. Before that, this Saturday, October 10, the drive-in returns to Juanita Beach Park: lot opens at 6 PM, $20 per vehicle, advance registration. Half marathon alert · Wheels & Reels

October 1, 2026

Saturday is the Harvest Festival on the north side of Juanita Beach Park, 11 to 4, and the drive-in returns to the same park on October 10. Longer term: the fieldhouse and community pool at Juanita High School are being upgraded under the district's 2024 levy, estimated completion 2028. Harvest Festival · LWSD construction

September 24, 2026

Juanita Beach Park reopened for swimming and wading on Tuesday, September 22, after a bacteria closure that began the 15th. It hosts the Harvest Festival on Saturday, October 3. City of Kirkland

Questions people ask

Is this a real estate newsletter?

No. It is a Kirkland newsletter that happens to know the housing market. Three of the four sections are about living here. The fourth tells you what homes are selling for, because in Kirkland that is news too.

Who writes it?

Neighborhood Press LLC, an independent publisher. Every item comes from Kirkland's own public record (council agendas, permit notices, school district and transit updates, parks calendars) and links its source. It is researched and written with AI tools, and an editor reads every issue before it goes out.

How often, and is it really free?

Once a week, Thursday at 6 AM, about five minutes to read. Free, no paywall, unsubscribe in one click at the bottom of any issue.

Who owns it?

Neighborhood Press LLC, an independent publishing company. The newsletter is free, and no sponsor buys coverage. Our standards.

What happens with my address?

It is used for one thing: a monthly estimate of what your home would sell for today. It goes to a local real estate brokerage under the same ownership as our publisher only if you tick the sharing box on the form; that brokerage may then contact you about your home. Leave the box empty and it stays with us. Never sold or posted. Remove it any time by replying to any issue.
