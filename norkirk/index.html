Questions people ask

Is this a real estate newsletter?

No. It is a Kirkland newsletter that happens to know the housing market. Three of the four sections are about living here. The fourth tells you what homes are selling for, because in Kirkland that is news too.

Who writes it?

Neighborhood Press LLC, an independent publisher. Every item comes from Kirkland's own public record (council agendas, permit notices, school district and transit updates, parks calendars) and links its source. It is researched and written with AI tools, and an editor reads every issue before it goes out.

How often, and is it really free?

Once a week, Thursday at 6 AM, about five minutes to read. Free, no paywall, unsubscribe in one click at the bottom of any issue.

Who owns it?

Neighborhood Press LLC, an independent publishing company. The newsletter is free, and no sponsor buys coverage. Our standards.

What happens with my address?

It is used for one thing: a monthly estimate of what your home would sell for today. It is shared with a local real estate brokerage under the same ownership as our publisher, which may contact you about your home, and is never sold or posted. Remove it any time by replying to any issue.
