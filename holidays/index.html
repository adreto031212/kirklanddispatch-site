Santa's Arrival Party at The Village at Totem Lake

Santa arrives at Totem Lake and stays for booked photo visits through Christmas Eve, with pet nights and two sensory-friendly sessions.

Village Green near Cinemark, The Village at Totem Lake, 11901 NE Village Plaza · Free party; Santa photos by booking · Organizer's page

Thu Nov 26 – Fri Jan 1 · Thanksgiving, Christmas Day, New Year's Day

Sound Transit holiday schedules

Link and ST Express run Sunday schedules on Thanksgiving, Christmas Day and New Year's Day; on Nov 27 Link runs a Saturday schedule and ST Express a weekday one.

Link light rail and ST Express · Regular fares · Organizer's page

Thu Nov 26 · All day

King County transfer stations closed on Thanksgiving

King County offices and its transfer stations and recycling facilities close on Thanksgiving Day; garbage pickup changes come from your own hauler.

King County solid waste facilities, including the Factoria Recycling and Transfer Station · None · Organizer's page
