Heads up

  • Planning Commission, City Hall and Zoom, tonight (Thursday, November 12) at 6 PM. A public hearing on this year's Comprehensive Plan amendments and on rezoning city-owned land to park (file CAM26-00726). Anyone may speak; written comments go in before 5 PM today. More below. Notice of hearing
  • Juanita Drive, Sunday, November 15, 8 to 11:30 AM. Eastbound Juanita Drive closes between 93rd and 98th Avenues NE for the Kirkland Turkey Trot, with a detour, and the Juanita Beach Park main lot entrance is closed from 8:30. Cars left in restricted areas will be towed. City traffic alert
  • 8231 124th Avenue NE, comments by Monday, November 16, 5 PM. An application to divide one 19,835-square-foot parcel into five unit lots (ULS26-00774). Written comments go to planner Alex Kaiser, AKaiser@kirklandwa.gov, with the file number. Notice of application
