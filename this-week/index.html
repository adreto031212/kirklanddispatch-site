Kirkland · Nov 26 to Dec 2

This week in Kirkland.

The dated things to know between now and next Thursday, from issue 10 of the Kirkland Dispatch. Every item links its public source.

What the city decided and what changed

Saturday, downtown and on the lake

November 28 is Small Business Saturday, and WinterFest, presented by Kirkland Urban and the Kirkland Downtown Association, brings the downtown tree lighting the same day.

The NE 85th station area is about to become the Forge District

The council's December 8 consent list includes formalizing the name for the area around the new I-405 Stride station at NE 85th Street.

What moved on December 8

The 5:30 PM slot is now a special meeting: the Kraken Iceplex and community center ground lease amendment, the Houghton Village purchase-and-sale agreement and interfund loan, and a Slate Peak…

Find your water shutoff before you need it

A full house means more showers, dishes and laundry, and a leak is far smaller if you can close the valve in a minute.

Why riders do not pick up after their horses on the trails when dog owners must

A reader on our Bridle Trails post asked why riders do not pick up after their horses on the trails when dog owners must.

On the calendar

Santa's Arrival Party at The Village at Totem Lake

Nov 21 to Dec 24 · 10:30 AM arrival party; Santa photos from 11 AM · Village Green near Cinemark, The Village at Totem Lake, 11901 NE Village Plaza. Santa arrives at Totem Lake and stays for booked photo visits through Christmas Eve, with pet nights and two sensory-friendly sessions. Organizer's page

Sound Transit holiday schedules

Nov 26 to Jan 1 · Thanksgiving, Christmas Day, New Year's Day · Link light rail and ST Express. Link and ST Express run Sunday schedules on Thanksgiving, Christmas Day and New Year's Day; on Nov 27 Link runs a Saturday schedule and ST Express a weekday one. Organizer's page

King County transfer stations closed on Thanksgiving

Nov 26 · All day · King County solid waste facilities, including the Factoria Recycling and Transfer Station. King County offices and its transfer stations and recycling facilities close on Thanksgiving Day; garbage pickup changes come from your own hauler. Organizer's page

Christmas Ship at O.O. Denny Park

Nov 28 · 2:35 to 2:55 PM · O.O. Denny Park. The Argosy Christmas Ship stops offshore for about 20 minutes while its choir sings carols over a speaker for people on the beach. Organizer's page

WinterFest at Kirkland Urban

Nov 28 · 3 to 7 PM · Kirkland Urban plaza, 425 Urban Plaza. Santa in his sleigh, ice carving, a snowfall on the promenade, kids' crafts, carolers and alpacas. Organizer's page

Kirkland Tree Lighting

Nov 28 · 6:15 PM · Peter Kirk Park. The Kirkland Downtown Association lights the town tree a short walk from WinterFest. Organizer's page
