The latest in Finn Hill

November 12, 2026

A pier replacement at 11837 Holmes Point Drive NE received the city's environmental sign-off, a Determination of Nonsignificance (SEP26-00410), on October 26. The old pier, pilings and covered moorage come out, and a new pier in a different spot carries the relocated boat lift and personal watercraft lift. The notice sets no comment period. SEPA notice

November 5, 2026

Nothing new on the city's news or calendar names Finn Hill this week. The November 4 and November 17 budget hearings cover the citywide capital list. Council calendar

October 29, 2026

The city's first community bonfire of the season is at O.O. Denny Park on Saturday, November 28, at 2:35 PM, timed to the Christmas Ship's stop. Bonfires can be called off for weather or a burn ban; the day-of hotline is 425-587-3345. City of Kirkland

October 22, 2026

The Green Loop now has a measured route: about 13.7 miles around Finn Hill, 6.3 of them already on the ground in some form. The rest needs new trail or easements, and private landowners would take part only if they choose to. The plan is due in January 2027. Big Finn Hill Park, 8106 NE 138th Street, also has a ballot drop box. Staff memo

October 15, 2026

The Green Loop, the planned trail and open-space corridor through Finn Hill and Juanita, gets its council update at the Tuesday, October 20 study session at 5:30 PM. The two Holmes Point Overlay items, trees and middle housing, are listed the same night. Council calendar · Green Loop

October 8, 2026

O.O. Denny Park, 12032 Holmes Point Drive NE, is 45.5 acres on the lake, owned by Seattle and run by Kirkland, with trails, Denny Creek and reservable picnic shelters; restrooms are seasonal, March through October. Northshore Utility District plans trench work on Holmes Point Drive by the park, date not yet set; when it starts, the park is reachable from one end only. City of Kirkland

October 1, 2026

The Green Loop, the planned trail and open-space corridor through Finn Hill and Juanita, is picking a logo: the survey closes Friday, October 2. Council gets a Green Loop update at its October 20 study session. Green Loop · Council calendar

September 24, 2026

Two Holmes Point Overlay items, one on middle-housing development rules and one on tree rules, are on council's list of upcoming business. No meeting date is attached yet. The overlay is the zoning layer that governs infill and environmental protection on the ridge. Council calendar

Questions people ask

Is this a real estate newsletter?

No. It is a Kirkland newsletter that happens to know the housing market. Three of the four sections are about living here. The fourth tells you what homes are selling for, because in Kirkland that is news too.

Who writes it?

Neighborhood Press LLC, an independent publisher. Every item comes from Kirkland's own public record (council agendas, permit notices, school district and transit updates, parks calendars) and links its source. It is researched and written with AI tools, and an editor reads every issue before it goes out.

How often, and is it really free?

Once a week, Thursday at 6 AM, about five minutes to read. Free, no paywall, unsubscribe in one click at the bottom of any issue.

Who owns it?

Neighborhood Press LLC, an independent publishing company. The newsletter is free, and no sponsor buys coverage. Our standards.

What happens with my address?

It is used for one thing: a monthly estimate of what your home would sell for today. It is shared with a local real estate brokerage under the same ownership as our publisher, which may contact you about your home, and is never sold or posted. Remove it any time by replying to any issue.
