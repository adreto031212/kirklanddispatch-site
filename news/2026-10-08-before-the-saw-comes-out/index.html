Kirkland · One useful thing

Before the saw comes out

October 8, 2026 · Kirkland Dispatch, issue 3 · All Kirkland news

Kirkland regulates any tree 6 inches or more in diameter, measured 4.5 feet up the trunk. A free online notification covers 2 removals every 12 months on lots up to 10,000 square feet, 3 up to 20,000 and 4 above that, if enough trees remain and the tree is under 26 inches (a Landmark tree). Critical areas, shoreline setbacks and the Holmes Point Overlay take a paid permit and an arborist's report. Topping is prohibited, and pruning over 25% of live canopy can count as removal. Questions: 425-587-3600. Tree Removal Guide · City trees page
