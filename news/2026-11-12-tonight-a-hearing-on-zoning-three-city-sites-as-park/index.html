Tonight: a hearing on zoning three city sites as park

November 12, 2026 · Kirkland Dispatch, issue 8 · All Kirkland news

The Planning Commission hears the city's proposal to rezone city-owned land from residential to park at three sites listed in the notice: 10454 Forbes Creek Drive, 10633 132nd Avenue NE and the church property at 13220 NE 132nd Street. The same hearing covers minor updates to the Comprehensive Plan's capital facilities and land use sections and to the Finn Hill Neighborhood Plan. The commission's recommendation goes to council, now set for December 8. Notice of hearing
