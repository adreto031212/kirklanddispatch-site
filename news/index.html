November 5, 2026

Council hears the budget on Wednesday, November 4

The study session opens at 5:30 PM and the regular meeting at 7:30 PM, City Hall, 123 5th Avenue.

The same night: a station name and minimum wage options

Council takes up naming the NE 85th Street station area and then minimum wage options as business items.

October 29, 2026

The 2027–28 budget is out, and council starts on it today

The city calls its preliminary budget balanced: current services kept, key infrastructure funded, and two years of operations added for the Kraken Iceplex and Kirkland Community Center.

November 4: a station name and minimum wage options

The 7:30 PM meeting opens hearings on the budget, the 2027–2032 capital plan and the preliminary 2027 property tax levy, then takes up naming the NE 85th Street station area and minimum wage options.

A Catrina procession on Halloween

The Kirkland Catrina Walk fills Marina Park, 25 Lakeshore Plaza, on Saturday, October 31, 11 AM to 8:30 PM, with the cultural procession at 6 PM. Free; Mundo Catrina presents it for the first time.

Recycle Right, Saturday, November 14

9 AM to 3 PM at Lake Washington Institute of Technology, 11605 132nd Avenue NE: electronics and small appliances, holiday lights, paper for shredding, clothing and linens.

Quieter mowing at one park

Parks crews are running electric mowers, edgers and blowers at Phyllis A. Needy Houghton Neighborhood Park in a one-year pilot, reviewed every quarter; it could expand to other parks.

Property taxes are due Monday this year

King County's second-half property tax is due October 31.

October 22, 2026

The average utility bill would rise 8.40% in 2027 and 8.35% in 2028

Final rates were on Tuesday's agenda for adoption: about $284 a month for a typical house in 2027, up $22.03.

Middle housing: quick fixes first

State law now allows at least four homes on every residential lot, but they follow single-family size rules.

Ballot drop boxes are open

Three boxes in Kirkland take ballots around the clock until 8 PM on Tuesday, November 3: the Wester Lot downtown, Big Finn Hill Park and Kingsgate Library.

Budget dates

Council's budget work session is Thursday, October 29, at 3 PM. On Wednesday, November 4, the first public hearings on the 2027–28 budget, the six-year capital plan and the 2027 property tax levy.

Shared scooters and e-bikes, a second look

Three councilmembers asked staff to scope a program; every city around Kirkland already has one.

Alarms and a way out, before the clocks change

Clocks fall back Sunday, November 1, an easy moment for a household fire check.

October 15, 2026

Drop, cover and hold on this morning at 10:15

Today is the Great ShakeOut, the statewide earthquake drill.

October 6: the church lease passed; the station name waits

Council allowed the newly acquired former church in Kingsgate to be leased for a community purpose while the city settles a long-term plan.

Tuesday, October 20: utility rates, then the budget

The 5:30 PM study session covers traffic signals, the Green Loop and a middle-housing briefing.

Northbound I-405 closes in Bellevue this weekend

All lanes between SE 8th Street and NE 4th Street close from 11 PM Friday, October 16, to 4 AM Monday, October 19, with the SE 8th on-ramp and the Exit 13 off-ramp closed.

Green Kirkland Week runs October 17 to 25

Volunteer stewardship events at parks across the city: planting, weeding and mulching to open the planting season.

Before the first windstorm

Report a power outage to Puget Sound Energy at 1-888-225-5773, pse.com/outage or the PSE app, not 911; a downed line is the 911 call.

October 8, 2026

Tuesday's council: speed limits, a station name, and a downtown housing agreement

Speed limits were a study session: discussion, no vote.

October 20: traffic signals, utility rates, and a Kraken lease that moved

The study session covers traffic signals and the Green Loop; the regular meeting lists utility-rate adoption, the faith-owned lands code and a middle-housing briefing.

Southbound I-405 closes in Bothell this weekend

All southbound lanes between SR 527 and SR 522 close from 10 PM Friday, October 9, to 4 AM Monday, October 12, with the NE 195th Street ramps closed.

A half marathon closes part of Juanita Drive on Saturday, October 17

About 1,000 runners start and finish at Juanita Beach Park; eastbound NE Juanita Drive closes 7 to 11:30 AM between 93rd and 98th Avenues NE. Cars parked on the course will be towed.

Halloween on the city calendar

The Pee Wee Monster Bash, for ages 1 to 5, is Friday, October 30, 10 AM to noon at the North Kirkland Community Center; $18 per child, register at KirklandParks.net or 425-587-3336.

Before the saw comes out

Kirkland regulates any tree 6 inches or more in diameter, measured 4.5 feet up the trunk.

October 1, 2026

The I-405 / NE 85th Street interchange closes tonight, then again all weekend

The shutdown called off in September is back on: 9 PM tonight, Thursday, October 1, to 5 AM Friday, then 10 PM Friday, October 2, to 5 AM Monday, October 5.

Tuesday, October 6: speed limits, a station name, and the Holmes Point overlay

The 5:30 PM study session takes up citywide speed-limit changes and an emergency management framework.

Harvest Festival, Saturday, October 3, 11 AM to 4 PM

North side of Juanita Beach Park, 9703 NE Juanita Drive, free: vendors, food trucks, a cornhole tournament, pony rides, and three bands starting at 11:30.

A drive-in at Juanita Beach on October 10

Wheels & Reels is back: Hoppers on a giant LED screen, lot opens at 6 PM, movie at 7, $20 per vehicle, advance registration required (kirklandparks.net or 425-587-3336).

Costume Swap, Saturday, October 10, 10 AM to noon, City Hall

Peter Kirk Room, lower lobby, lake side; free; bringing one is optional.

September 24, 2026

I-405 at NE 132nd Street closes this weekend

The northbound and southbound on- and off-ramps at NE 132nd Street close from 10 PM Friday, September 25, to 5 AM Monday, September 28, and NE 132nd Street under the freeway closes with them.

The weekend shutdown of the I-405 / NE 85th Street interchange is still on hold

WSDOT cancelled the full closure it had planned for September 18–21 "until further notice" and says it will give advance notice of the new date; as of September 24 no new date has been announced.

Tuesday, October 6: speed limits and a name for the station area

Council's 5:30 PM study session takes up citywide speed-limit changes.

Harvest Festival, Saturday, October 3, at Juanita Beach Park

11 AM to 4 PM on the north side of the park, 9703 NE Juanita Drive.

No school Friday, October 16

Lake Washington School District has a professional-development day on the 16th, with preschool conferences October 13–15.

Metro's fall schedule is in effect

Route 249, which serves South Kirkland Park & Ride, now runs through Medina, Hunts Point and Clyde Hill instead of Bellevue Way; the new Route 270 covers Bellevue Way.
