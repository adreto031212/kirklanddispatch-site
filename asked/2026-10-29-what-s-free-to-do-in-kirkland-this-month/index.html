What's free to do in Kirkland this month

October 29, 2026 · Kirkland Dispatch, issue 6 · Every answered question

A reader in Totem Lake asked what's free to do in Kirkland this month. Here are the dated, free or drop-in picks on the city's own calendar through November. Saturday, October 31: the Catrina Walk at Marina Park, 11 AM to 8:30 PM, procession at 6. Sunday, November 1: Green Kirkland work mornings at Neal-Landguth Park (10 to noon) and South Norway Hill Park (11:30 to 1:30), tools provided, registration requested. Monday, November 9: Conversations with Council, 5 to 7 PM at the Northwest University café. Saturday, November 14: Recycle Right at Lake Washington Institute of Technology, 9 to 3, the closest to Totem Lake. City events · Community events page Know a neighbor asking the same thing?
