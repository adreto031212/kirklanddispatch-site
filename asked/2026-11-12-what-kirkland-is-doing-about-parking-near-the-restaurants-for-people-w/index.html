What Kirkland is doing about parking near the restaurants for people who cannot walk far

November 12, 2026 · Kirkland Dispatch, issue 8 · Every answered question

A reader downtown also asked what Kirkland is doing about parking near the restaurants for people who cannot walk far. Accessible stalls sit on Central Way (4), Kirkland Avenue (3), Lake Street (2), Park Lane (2), Lake Avenue West (1) and Main Street (1), with 9 in the Peter Kirk garage, and the city is piloting a real-time map of open spaces. With a disability placard or plate displayed, state law lets you park free, with no time limit, in metered or time-limited spots.
